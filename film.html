Film Portfolio

This portfolio showcases a selection of short movies that I have written, directed, filmed, and edited. Most of my films are abstract, atmospheric, and experimental, not usually conforming to traditional narrative structures. One of my films won the Best Experimental Film award at the 2025 Burr and Burton Academy Film Festival. I have also received two Effort and Achievement Awards in the Media Arts.

To view my written works and artworks, see the portfolio directory.

"The River of Windfall Light," winner of the Best Experimental Film award at the 2025 Burr and Burton Academy Film Festival
