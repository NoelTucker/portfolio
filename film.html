Film Portfolio

This portfolio showcases a selection of short films that were written, directed, shot, and edited by myself. Most of my films are abstract, atmospheric, and experimental, and do not conform to traditional narrative structures. Some have been shown at Burr & Burton Academy's annual film festival and awards show, where one recieved the Best Experimental Film award. I have also received academic honors in Media Arts from Burr & Burton Academy.

To view my written works and artwork, see the portfolio directory.

"The River of Windfall Light," winner of the Best Experimental Film award at the 2025 Burr & Burton Academy Film Festival
"A Picture Book of Memories," a collection of family photos and videos set to "Picture Book" by the Kinks
"Magic and Beauty," a tribute to Robert Louis Stevenson
"Child of Air," an art film about growing up
