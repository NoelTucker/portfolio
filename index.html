I'm Noel Tucker, a writer, filmmaker, and artist residing in rural upstate New York. I am currently a senior at Burr and Burton Academy—a high school in southern Vermont—and will be attending Bennington College in the fall. My work has been printed in BBA's student newspaper and literary magazine, won an award at the BBA film festival, and been chosen for display at the Southern Vermont Arts Center.
